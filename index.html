The single biggest source of confusion

Three gate systems. Same labels. Different meanings.

NSE runs three independent, sequentially-numbered "gate" systems side by side. A project or demand can be at "G2" in all three at once — and each G2 means something completely different, owned by a different person.

Try it: press the button to see every "G2" light up across all three systems at the same time.

Rule of thumb for SNIP: never show a bare gate number in the UI without naming which of these three systems it belongs to.
